### Hello, I'm Waylon

I'm an engineer at the San Francisco Compute Company. I work primarily in Rust, with experience in systems engineering, simulation, and applied mathematics.

I like turning mathematical ideas into working software, understanding how systems behave, and making complicated code easier to reason about. I'm especially interested in infrastructure and engineering problems that benefit from research.

A few examples of my work:

- [TCP congestion simulation](https://github.com/0xJepsen/TropicalAlgebraicTCPModel): built a discrete-event simulator to evaluate a tropical max-plus model of TCP across network topologies and parameters.
- [Tropical algebra in Rust](https://github.com/harnesslabs/cova/pull/19): contributed semiring and semimodule abstractions, a tropical algebra implementation, documentation, and tests.
- [Web Prover](https://github.com/pluto/web-prover/pull/264): improved error handling across the Rust client and proof-generation code, replacing many unchecked operations with explicit error propagation.

My interests still sit at the intersection of mathematics, computer science, and philosophy. I spent my first 13 years growing up off-grid in a Tibetan Buddhist community in the Nyingma tradition. I care about the people I work with and the teams we build together.
